About Me

I am a researcher passionate about interdisciplinary studies, dedicated to integrating knowledge from human factors engineering, cognitive psychology, and design science to address challenges in information visualization design. Over the past five years, my research has focused on visualization methods that enhance human–machine collaborative decision-making, during which I have participated in multiple collaborative projects with leading domestic enterprises. Based on a series of physiological experiments, my research provides theoretical support for scientific approaches to information visualization, guiding the representation methods of complex information systems.

I possess a strong sense of responsibility and excellent communication skills. I am highly execution-oriented, capable of completing assigned tasks efficiently and with high quality, while providing regular progress reports to ensure alignment. I have strong organizational and coordination abilities, extensive experience, and have served as an organizer and coordinator on multiple occasions. I am detail-oriented and adapt well to multi-tasking and dynamic challenges.
