Teaching

Courses taught at Southeast University. Click semester links for course materials.

Data Visualization ME 441 / 541

An introduction to the principles and techniques of data visualization. Topics include visual encoding, interaction design, perception, and evaluation methods. Students complete a semester-long visualization project.

Human-Computer Interaction ME 485 / 584

Covers the design, implementation, and evaluation of interactive systems. Topics include user research, prototyping, usability testing, and accessibility. Students conduct a user study as part of the course project.

Spring 2025 (32 students) | Spring 2024 (29 students)

Introduction to Machine Learning ME 371

Introduces fundamental machine learning algorithms and their applications. Topics include supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and ethical considerations in AI systems.
