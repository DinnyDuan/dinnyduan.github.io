About Me

PH.D. STUDENT
SCHOOL OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING
SOUTHEAST UNIVERSITY

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Southeast University. My research focuses on cognition and visualization. I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at SEU in 2025.

Prospective students interested in working with me should review my research and reach out via email with your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Research Interests

My research investigates the intersection of human cognition and data visualization, with a focus on designing intelligent tools that support decision-making and analytical workflows. I develop computational strategies to characterize human limitations and design interventions to promote reflective data analysis processes.
