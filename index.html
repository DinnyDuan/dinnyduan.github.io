About Me

PH.D. STUDENT
SCHOOL OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING
SOUTHEAST UNIVERSITY

I am a researcher passionate about interdisciplinary studies, dedicated to integrating knowledge from human factors engineering, cognitive psychology, and design science to address challenges in information visualization design. Over the past five years, my research has focused on visualization methods that enhance human–machine collaborative decision-making, during which I have participated in multiple collaborative projects with leading domestic enterprises.

Based on a series of physiological experiments, my research provides theoretical support for scientific approaches to information visualization, guiding the representation methods of complex information systems.

Research Interests

Human-Computer Interaction  ·  Complex Information System Visualization  ·  Ergonomics  ·  Human Factors Engineering  ·  Data Visualization  ·  Cognitive Psychology
